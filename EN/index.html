      How the measurement works
      1. Choose a person and check the settings.
      2. Setup check: light, face, distance and steadiness must be green.
      3. Calibration: a dot jumps to the centre, left, right, top and bottom. Follow it with your eyes only. The measurement only starts once the calibration is OK.
      4. Measurement: the stripe pattern runs in each direction at each speed. Look at the stripes in a relaxed way. Tap to pause.
      5. Result: gain (eye velocity ÷ stripe speed), beat frequency, amplitude, asymmetry. Then export: Share → Save to Files, AirDrop or SeaFile.

      Tip: via Share → Add to Home Screen the app runs full screen. Safari may delete website data after a long period of non-use, so export measurements regularly. The German and English versions share the same measurements on this device.
